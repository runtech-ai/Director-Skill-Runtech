# Author, review, repair, recheck

Use this before compiling each segment and before handing off a complete pack. It is a Director content pass, separate from the official H3 form pass. A valid H3 structure can still direct the wrong action.

Apply [media-lifecycle.md](media-lifecycle.md) during every repair/retry: keep evidence on disk, inspect bounded batches, checkpoint compact findings and release media buffers before advancing. Whole-pack recurrence checks operate on text records and targeted evidence retrieval, not one accumulated collection of movie media.

## Author only the current beat

For each important object in each micro-shot, keep a compact project-local ledger: source beat and authority; visible or concealed; holder/support/contact; entry and exit state; permitted motion and its initiating cause; effect emitter, extent, onset and end. Explicitly record an ordinary stationary state or no effect when appropriate. Preserve importer fields; the ledger is supporting validation evidence, not a replacement schema.

Distinguish object rotation from camera orbit, translation from axial rotation, reflected highlights from emitted light, and an energy/spirit effect from a solid component. A reference sheet establishes design, not current motion, glow, staging or population. A plot-relevant prop need not move to remain visually interesting.

Use a concise positive state and only the restrictions needed to disambiguate that beat. Do not paste a movie-wide mechanics checklist into every shot. A handling shot does not inherit launch, landing, collision, spin blur or manifestation instructions. Mixed outcomes need separate per-object instructions; a generic successful mechanism must not overwrite a scripted failure.

Every motion or effect needs source authorization and a valid cause under the applicable project rules. A dream/title/symbolic setting is not an automatic exemption. If a higher-priority rule excludes the original depiction, preserve its narrative purpose through permitted coverage, record the adaptation, and synchronize the camera and board. Do not silently invent a cause or erase an explicitly authorized supernatural event.

Keep visual event instructions in picture direction. Music fields describe audible instrumentation, pace and dynamics, never a prop brightening, rotating or manifesting. Ambient/foley fields cannot imply an unpictured launch or collision. Ordinary specular reflections do not authorize a glow, aura, trail, sparks or sustained beam. Scope an authorized effect to its emitter and time window, with its outgoing state defined.

## Close the repair loop upstream

1. Review the complete segment against raw source, current canonical assets and the ledger. Check dialogue occupancy, identities/population, support, causality, camera, transitions and neighboring boundary states. Perform a fresh review without relying on the writer's pass claim; use an independent reviewer when available and authorized.
2. Record each defect with its exact field/shot or observed frame/time, expected state, actual state, and source. Classify `PROMPT_ONLY`, `STORYBOARD_RESHOOT`, or `FULL_SEGMENT_REDIRECT`. Word searches locate candidates; they cannot determine whether a reflection, manual turn or authored effect is wrong.
3. Fix the earliest cause in the authoritative Director record. Replace conflicting prose rather than append another lock. Synchronize segment JSON, scene plan/index and legacy copies, per-segment and aggregate bundles, shot-plan text, storyboard specs/prompts and continuity handoffs. Remove duplicate and obsolete instructions. Preserve exact structured dialogue and unaffected authored coverage.
4. Validate the repaired content, then run official H3 form validation. Recheck attachment labels after either repair. If rendered boards disagree, generate fresh boards from current canonical assets and corrected direction, then inspect their pixels. A corrected prompt does not make an old board correct.
5. Where generation is already authorized, review the exact newly generated take, repair only evidence-backed defects and retry. Inspect full visual coverage and denser samples around causal events. Apply the user's current audio gate; verify exact dialogue and silent-window occupancy when mandatory, and record explicitly waived checks as `not_required_by_user`. Do not claim listening or a media pass without evidence.
6. Rerun failed checks and checks affected by the repair, including adjacent segments and redundant copies. Then recheck the whole pack for recurrence of the same defect class. Reopen final archives and validate their contents, not just the source directory.

Handoff requires no unresolved applicable failures. Record coverage, fixes, remaining unknowns, source references and evidence paths; distinguish text validation from pixel, runtime and generated-video validation. Never mark an untested layer passed. Preserve correct segments and media. Do not retry an unchanged prompt blindly: repeated failures require a different supported representation or simpler usable coverage. Continue local repair until convergence; stop external retries on user stop, actual authority/infrastructure failure or an explicit cost/run limit and record the exact resume action. This procedure does not itself authorize new paid generation, publishing, or deployment.

Store reusable lessons here only when they generalize. Keep franchise mechanics, effect permissions, IDs and active findings in the project, and route future authors to that authority.
