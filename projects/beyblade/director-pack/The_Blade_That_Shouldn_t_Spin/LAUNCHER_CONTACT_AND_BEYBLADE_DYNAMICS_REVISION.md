# Launcher contact and Beyblade dynamics revision

- Loaded tops now touch the launcher underside with no visible gap and move rigidly with it until the single release frame.
- No rod, shaft, stem, cable, spacer or other visible connector may appear between launcher housing and top.
- Ripcord torque accelerates a successful top while it is still attached; its first detached frame is already high-RPM and motion-blurred.
- After release, RPM cannot rise from zero or restart without a photographed external torque; failed releases remain stopped and fall.
- High RPM now requires circumferential texture blur rather than crisp, frozen perimeter markings.
- Tilt now produces continuously rotating precession rather than a fixed lean.
- Stadium travel follows continuous curved trajectories and may not freeze, teleport, or form a staged row.
- Rim collisions now require immediate separation, deflection or rebound, wobble, and energy loss; magnetic clustering and overlap are forbidden.
- Frictional decay ends in widening precession, scraping, and a stopped top falling onto its side.
- Combined identity plates may not impose their catalogue arrangement on generated battle staging.
