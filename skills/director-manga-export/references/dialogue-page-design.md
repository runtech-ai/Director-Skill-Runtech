# Dialogue and page design: approved vertical comic layout

Use this layout for both manhwa and manga unless the user requests another format. Read vertically from top to bottom. ## Layout grammar

- Center artwork within consistent white side margins. Vary panel height and framing according to the source beat instead of using a dense uniform grid.
- Reserve substantial white canvas above, below and between art panels for dialogue and silent pauses. White space is part of the composition, not waste to crop away.
- Place large rounded white balloons with clean black outlines mainly in the reserved white regions. Allow selected balloons to overlap panel edges without masking faces or decisive action.
- Link successive balloons from the same speaker with a smooth joined shape or clear connector. Stagger them down the page to make reading order unmistakable. Never connect different speakers as one reply.
- Point the tail to the actual speaker, even when the balloon is outside the art. Preserve off-panel delivery and listener silence.
- Use a generous pause after a dialogue group before a major reveal or impact where the source supports it.
- Let a source-supported weapon, arm, silhouette or effect extend outside a panel into a planned gutter selectively. Maintain anatomy and causality; do not force an impact scene or new action solely to demonstrate the design.
- Keep border breaks and speech regions separate. Do not allow an effect to obscure words or create a misleading speaker connection.
- Scale dialogue to comfortable mobile reading size with generous padding and natural phrase breaks. Add canvas or pages before reducing type. Two panels are an example, not a mandatory count.

## Lettering and source fidelity

Use black comic lettering on opaque white balloons. For English and other suitable Latin-script dialogue, favor an uppercase-looking hand-lettered comic face. Preserve the exact source string, punctuation, language and speaker in the ledger. Prefer a font that renders uppercase-looking glyphs without changing the stored string; record any visual case transform and preserve case where meaning depends on it. Other languages require their own glyph coverage and shaping, not transliteration or forced capitals.

Split a long source line only at natural phrase boundaries; its balloon pieces must reconstruct the source once in order, apart from layout whitespace and explicitly recorded visual case treatment. Never invent dialogue to fill the gutters. Preserve authored emphasis and silence. Place only source-supported sound effects in expressive brush lettering near their actual event, visually distinct from speech.

Prefer deterministic editable lettering over text baked into generated art. Generated text is acceptable only after every word, punctuation mark, speaker tail and placement is checked. Keep a balloon-to-source-line mapping regardless of rendering route.

## Rendering by mode

**Manhwa:** full color, precise dark linework, controlled cel shading, clear silhouettes, consistent palette and lighting. The example's muted fantasy colors are not mandatory for unrelated source settings.

**Manga:** black ink, paper white, distinguishable screentones, varied line weights, selective hatching and solid black shadows. Translate canonical color identities consistently into values/patterns. Redraw the treatment as manga instead of simply applying grayscale; inspect reduced-size tones for moiré. Keep the same dialogue and layout rules.

## Plan and acceptance

Record layout preset, reading flow, panel bounds, balloon bounds, connected group IDs, speaker/tail targets, source-line mappings, border-break extents and safe export cuts in the internal comic plan. No new RunTech importer keys are required.

Check the complete composition at mobile width and final PDF size:
1. Exact source coverage and dialogue ownership, with no omitted or repeated lines.
2. Readable lettering, padding, natural breaks and correct glyphs.
3. Unambiguous tails and connected-balloon reading order.
4. Deliberate white pauses and varied panel rhythm.
5. Border-breaking art fully visible, anatomically valid and clear of text.
6. Color/ink treatment appropriate to the selected mode.
7. PDF cuts preserve whole dialogue groups and action extents; no tall-strip shrink-to-fit.
