# Beyblade direction profile

Project instance of the template in [genre direction profiles](../../../global/skills/director-pack-skills/references/genre-direction-profiles.md). Identity, wardrobe and physics invariants live in `../h3/h3-locks.json` and the Physics Bible, not here.

1. **Primary genre and registers:** family sports anime. *Everyday register* for home, street and school scenes; *match register* for launches and battles; *spirit register* for the Bit Beast and dream imagery.
2. **Medium:** 2D TV anime with stepped character animation.
3. **Format:** 16:9, segments up to 15 s, 600 s film.
4. **Camera baseline:** physical camera logic in every register. Everyday: eye-level, static layouts or short slides, small acting inside held frames. Match: low angles on players, diagonal layouts, follow pans on travelling tops, a top-down only when the shot is authored as overhead. No camera move without a trigger.
5. **Coverage defaults:**
   - Everyday dialogue: establishing two-shot when someone enters or the geography changes, then shot / reverse-shot with one line per speaking shot. Eema stands and Kaiden sits, so her singles look slightly down from his eyeline and his look slightly up from hers.
   - Reactions: Kaiden's reactions get the close shots; he is the scene's point of view at home.
   - Inserts: the top, the washer, the phone screen, each motivated by Kaiden's look.
   - Match: launch sequence as hand insert → player low angle → top landing; exchanges cut between player, top and opponent; hold the result wide.
6. **Rhythm:** everyday 2.5–5 s; match 1–2 s, with one held beat before a decisive clash.
7. **Performance:** grounded anime acting. Kaiden hides feelings in busy hands (turning the top, picking at the washer); Eema is dry, patient and physically calm.
8. **Sound and music:** monsoon rain, fan hum and room tone at home; crowd, wind and spin whine at matches. Score cues are continuous per scene.
9. **Deliberate exceptions:** opening-title tableau and dream void may use a stylized static composition.
