## Already-spinning storyboard images — mandatory visual gate

When the Director's selected beat already has a Beyblade spinning, render that ongoing rotation in the image itself. A crisp stationary catalogue pose with the word “spinning” only in the prompt is a failed board: it gives the video model a stopped-looking state from which to invent spin-up. For an already-spinning beat with no explicitly authored lower-RPM state, depict high RPM. Preserve explicitly authored medium/low RPM, wobble, deceleration and stopped outcomes rather than upgrading them to high RPM.

- High RPM must show strong circumferential motion blur on the Attack Ring/perimeter and rotationally smeared outer markings. Preserve the palm-sized rigid silhouette, canonical color bands/component stack, coherent axis and readable support/contact. The centred emblem may remain visually centred; the perimeter cannot be wholly sharp and frozen.
- Apply rotational blur to the moving top, not the entire frame. Camera blur, a blurred background, translation streaks alone, an aura, glowing halo or detached duplicate rings do not prove axial rotation. Do not add supernatural shine or effects to communicate speed.
- Select an instant in the authored ongoing-spin phase, not an earlier stationary/pre-pull state. A successful release or rotating descent shows rotation already established; it cannot depict a crisp motionless top and rely on the video prompt to start it later. Keep tip contact for grounded spin and the correct unsupported descent state for an airborne release.
- Evaluate each top separately. Already-spinning tops need the correct RPM cues; a failed, held, stored, loaded-before-pull, stopped or ejected-and-stopped top stays in its authored non-spinning state. Never smear all tops identically because one is spinning. A stationary canonical sheet supplies design, not permission to freeze a spinning board or spin a stopped top.
- Put the current RPM band and visible rotation cue into the owning beat/spec and image prompt, then verify the actual board pixels. In the panel review record, save `expected RPM/state -> observed perimeter cue -> support/contact -> pass/fail/unknown`, with the exact board hash and evidence region. A static-looking perimeter fails an expected high-RPM board; obscured or unreadable required cues remain unknown. Neither silently passes.
- Repair the relevant prompt/spec and regenerate fresh from canonical references when images are authorized, then recheck all panels and per-top states. Do not accept a stopped-looking board on the promise that H3 will fix it. A passing still verifies its depicted rotation cues only; continuous RPM, direction and motion causality remain exact-video review gates.
